# Comprehensive Development Prompt for Nepal IPO Analysis & Recommendation App

## Project Overview
Create a comprehensive mobile and web application that helps Nepali investors make informed decisions about IPO investments in Nepal's stock market. The app should analyze upcoming IPOs, provide recommendations based on financial data and market trends, and operate within Nepal's regulatory framework.

## Target Market
- Nepali investors (both novice and experienced)
- Focus on Nepal's unique market conditions and regulations
- Support for both urban users with good connectivity and rural users with limited connectivity

## Core Features and Requirements

### 1. IPO Discovery & Calendar
- Real-time tracking of upcoming, ongoing, and recently closed IPOs in Nepal
- Detailed calendar with key dates (opening, closing, minimum shares, allotment dates)
- Notification system for important IPO events
- Filter options based on sector, issue size, and subscription type (public, mutual funds, staff)

### 2. Company Analysis Module
- Comprehensive company profiles including:
  - Business overview, industry position, and competitive advantages
  - Management team analysis and track record
  - Financial statement analysis (3-5 years if available)
  - Key financial ratios relevant to Nepal's market (P/E, P/B, ROE, Debt-to-Equity)
  - Intended use of IPO proceeds
  - Group/parent company relationships and business connections

### 3. Recommendation Engine
- Multi-factor analysis algorithm with:
  - Fundamental score (based on financial health)
  - Valuation score (issue price compared to peers and fundamentals)
  - Sector outlook score (growth potential of industry in Nepal)
  - Management quality score
  - Market timing score (based on current market conditions)
- Clear recommendation categories: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Avoid
- Probability estimation for short-term listing gains
- Long-term investment potential assessment
- Transparent explanation of recommendation factors

### 4. Nepal Market Intelligence
- Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) index tracking and correlation with IPO performance
- Sector-specific performance indicators
- Historical IPO database with subscription rates and listing performances
- Regulatory news and updates from SEBON and Nepal Rastra Bank
- Sentiment analysis of Nepali financial news from major sources

### 5. Educational Resources
- Step-by-step IPO application guide for Nepal's ASBA/C-ASBA system
- DEMAT account setup instructions
- IPO investment basics tailored to Nepal's market
- Frequently asked questions about Nepal's IPO process
- Glossary of Nepali market-specific terms

### 6. User Personalization
- User profile with investment preferences and risk tolerance
- Watchlist for tracking specific upcoming IPOs
- Portfolio tracking for allotted shares
- Performance tracking of investments
- Customizable notification settings

## Technical Requirements

### Data Sources
- Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) website and API

### Frontend Development
- Progressive Web App (PWA) architecture for cross-platform compatibility
- Mobile-first responsive design optimized for popular Nepali devices
- Support for low-bandwidth mode for users in areas with poor connectivity
- Offline capability for basic information viewing
- Modern UI framework (React/React Native) with Nepali language support
- Visualization libraries for financial data representation

### Data Analysis Components
- Natural Language Processing for Nepali financial news sentiment analysis
- Rule-based scoring algorithm with transparent weighting
- Time-series analysis for historical pattern recognition
- Simple machine learning models for subscription rate prediction
- Financial ratio calculation engine

### Regulatory Compliance
- Adherence to SEBON guidelines for investment advice
- Clear disclaimers about investment risks
- Transparency in data sources and analysis methodology
- Privacy protection for user data according to Nepal's data protection laws
- Terms of service aligned with Nepali financial sector regulations

4. How to Apply for IPOs in Nepal
To participate in IPOs, you’ll need:

Demat Account: Open a Demat account with a registered stockbroker or financial institution.
Mero Share: Use the Mero Share platform to apply for IPOs online.
Log in, go to “My ASBA” > “Current Issue,” select the IPO, enter the quantity, and apply.
ASBA System: Ensure your bank account is linked for the Application Supported by Blocked Amount (ASBA) process, which blocks funds until allotment.
IPO Result Checker: Check allotment results on CDSC’s IPO Result Checker or the issue manager’s website.
5. Additional Tips
Stay Updated: IPO schedules change frequently. Regularly check SEBON’s website or subscribe to financial portals like ShareSansar or NepseBuzz for notifications.
Research Companies: Before investing, review company financials, sector performance, and growth potential. SEBON’s IPO documents or portals like Investopaper provide this data.
Premium IPOs: Some IPOs, like Ghorahi Cement Industry Limited (premium price Rs. 435, face value Rs. 100), are issued above face value. Understand the share premium concept before investing.
Sector Trends: In 2081 (2025), expect IPOs in hydropower, tourism, and microfinance. Hydropower IPOs are particularly significant due to Nepal’s water resources, but delays in SEBON approvals may affect timelines.
